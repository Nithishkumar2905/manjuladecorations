Crafting Memories Since Inception

Manjula Decorations began with a simple passion: to bring the rich, vibrant essence of South Indian traditions into life's greatest celebrations.

Based near the Collector Office in Tirupattur, we have transformed countless venues into breathtaking, memorable spaces over the years. We blend traditional aesthetics, like marigold garlands and temple-style mandaps, with modern, luxurious lighting and structural elegance.
